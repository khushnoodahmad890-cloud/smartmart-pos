FAQ

Common questions

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The desktop edition runs fully on your own PC — sales continue even with no internet. The cloud edition also queues sales offline and syncs automatically when the connection returns.

Do I need special hardware?

No. It works with any normal PC or laptop, any USB barcode scanner and standard 80mm/58mm thermal receipt printers. You can even scan barcodes with a phone camera.

Can my staff steal or hide sales?

Every action is logged with the user's name and time. Cashiers get limited permissions, cash drawers are reconciled with Z-reports, and unusual refunds or discounts are flagged automatically.

Is my data safe? Who owns it?

You do — always. On the desktop edition everything stays on your own computer with one-click backups. On the cloud edition each store has its own isolated database, and you can export everything to Excel at any time.

Can you help me set it up?

Yes — setup, product import from Excel, barcode labels, printer configuration and staff training are all included with paid plans and desktop licenses.
